Southern Noir · Mystery Thriller

Beneath the Foxglove

By G.K. Boni · Cover Design by Riz the Wizz
"In a village full of secrets, even beauty can kill."
In Peki, secrets don’t stay buried. They push up through the red earth—bright, poisonous, and sharp as regret. Detective Fafa Bankas—former beauty queen, now wearing suspicion like a second skin—comes home to a village choking on silence. Here, poison smiles and every handshake hides a blade. In Peki, the truth doesn’t set you free; it puts you in the ground.

Children's Illustrated Book

Carboot Karma

Illustrated by: Riz Wan (Riz the Wizz)
"Kindness always finds its way back home."
Aiden spots a pair of dream trainers in a shop window, but knowing his family's budget is tight, he sets up a blanket stall at the local car boot sale. Selling his old toys and learning to count change, Aiden earns enough—only to spend his coins on a needed school uniform for his little sister. A heartwarming tale of generosity and karma.
